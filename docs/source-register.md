## Rules for including a source

1. The source is published and legally usable.
2. The author(s), publication year, and publication venue are known and verifiable.
3. The source falls within the approved research topic: **Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Agentic RAG)**.
4. Every extracted claim can be traced to an exact evidence snippet in the original source.

## Known coverage gaps

- Questions outside the Agentic RAG research domain.
- Research published after the approved corpus was created.
- Papers that are not part of the approved corpus, even if they are relevant to Agentic RAG.
- Proprietary, confidential, or unpublished research.
- Real-time information, news, or whole-web search results.
