## Rules for including a source

1. It is published and legally usable.
2. Its author/organisation and date are known.
3. It is inside the chosen narrow topic.
4. Every claim we use can be traced back to an exact snippet in the original.

## Known coverage gaps

Cannot answer questions regarding specific proprietary enterprise Agentic RAG frameworks or benchmarks outside of the broad survey data. Does not include domain-specific (e.g. purely medical or legal) autonomous agents unless covered in the multimodal surveys.

## Domain Taxonomy Examples

The following question types define our AI/RAG domain vocabulary:

- **method**: "What is the GraphRAG approach to summarization?" (Querying specific algorithms/techniques).
- **finding**: "What are the limitations of System 1 vs System 2 Reasoning?" (Querying results/outcomes).
- **comparison**: "How does Agentic RAG compare to standard RAG?" (Evaluating differences between architectures).
- **gap**: "What research is missing in Multimodal Agentic RAG?" (Identifying unknown areas).
